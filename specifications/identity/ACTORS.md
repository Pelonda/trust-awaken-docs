# Identity Actors

## Platform User

A user who can access the Trust AWAKEN platform administration area.

### Typical capabilities
- Create organizations
- Manage subscriptions
- View audits
- Support customers
- Manage system settings

## Organization User

A user who belongs to one organization and manages that organization’s operations.

### Typical capabilities
- Manage organization settings
- Manage users
- Manage templates
- Issue documents
- View reports

## Recipient

A person who receives a credential, certificate, diploma, badge, transcript, or similar record.

### Typical capabilities
- Usually no login required
- May view or verify their own records
- May access verification links
